# Nexus Launcher Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 17 October 2026**

Nexus Launcher is a personal Android launcher project. This policy explains what information may be processed by launcher features and how Android permissions affect that processing.

## Information processed on your device
The launcher processes settings, home-screen preferences, selected apps and configuration needed to provide its features. App names and launchable activities may be read to display the app drawer and search results. This information is generally processed on your device.

## Digital Wellbeing and Accessibility
If you enable Usage Access, Digital Wellbeing reads Android usage statistics to calculate app usage limits. If you enable the Nexus Launcher Accessibility service, it observes app-window changes so selected distracting apps can be interrupted during a focus session. These permissions are optional for the launcher overall. You can revoke them in Android Settings.

## Other permissions
Features may request access to media, audio, contacts, biometrics or network connectivity when needed. Android permission prompts and system settings control these grants. Do not grant a permission unless you understand why the feature needs it.

## Accounts and online services
If an optional account, cloud, AI, voice, search or other online integration is enabled, information you submit may be sent to the selected provider to fulfil the request. The provider's own privacy terms apply. Do not submit sensitive information unless the relevant feature requires it and you trust the provider.

## Third-party links
The launcher may open third-party websites, apps, media providers or learning resources. Those services are independent and may collect information under their own policies.

## Sharing and advertising
This project does not intentionally sell personal information. Data may be processed by a third-party service when you choose an integration, or disclosed when required by law. This statement does not override the privacy terms of services you independently enable.

## Retention and security
Local preferences remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the launcher. Online services may retain data according to their own settings and policies. Keep your device updated and avoid sharing logs containing personal information.

## Your choices
You can review or revoke Android permissions, disable optional features and stop using integrations. Some features will not work without related permissions.

## Changes and contact
This policy may change as the project evolves. For questions or corrections, use the issue tracker at [github.com/Kuldeep532/lawnchair](https://github.com/Kuldeep532/lawnchair). Do not post private information in public issues.
